The source file contains 5,711 non-empty Vietnamese sentences. The VN Office
word list was derived offline by normalizing the corpus to Unicode NFC,
extracting alphabetic tokens, converting them to lowercase, removing exact
duplicates, and sorting the resulting 2,798 distinct tokens with the
Vietnamese locale. No LibreOffice, Hunspell,
Ho Ngoc Duc, Wiktionary, OpenSubtitles, or other dictionary data is included.
