# Shared Platform Standard
Đưa vào Platform nếu capability:
- dùng >=2 app; hoặc
- là shell/system concern; hoặc
- cần visual/behavior consistency toàn suite.

Platform examples: button, dropdown, gallery, font picker, color picker, file menu, ribbon host, dialogs, theme, notifications, command palette.

Không đưa vào Platform: formula semantics, paragraph-specific business logic, slide animation logic.
