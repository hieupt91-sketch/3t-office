## Ket luan lop W26

Lop **W26 (drift:empty-para)** gom hai nhom:

(a) **Than (document.xml)** — Adapter co san `document.xml` de doc truc tiep `w:pPr/w:rPr/w:sz` va chain style. Co the tinh toan cuo dau doan chuan xac cho moi doan rong. Can mo rong adapter de tra ve `empty_para_size` cho tung doan.

(b) **Header/Footer (header*.xml, footer*.xml)** — Adapter **chua nhan** header/footer XML. Can `main.py` trich xuat va truyen `ooxmlHeaderXml` / `ooxmlFooterXml` (tuong tu `document.xml`) vao adapter de xu ly doan rong o header/footer cung co che.

De xuat: mo rong `VN LibreOffice Adapter` them field `empty_para_size` trong ket qua parse, va cap nhat `main.py` de load header/footer XML khi mo file.
