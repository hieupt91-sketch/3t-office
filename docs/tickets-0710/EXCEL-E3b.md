# E3b - Ctrl+Shift+; chen GIO nhu GIA TRI SO (Excel 2016)
Khoang trong con lai tu E3: Ctrl+Shift+; trong VN Sheet chen CHUOI 'HH:mm:ss'. Excel 2016 chen GIA TRI SO (serial thoi gian, phan le cua ngay) co dinh dang gio (h:mm theo cai dat he thong, vi du 'h:mm AM/PM' hoac 'h:mm'), nen tinh toan duoc (cong/tru gio) va hien dung o tab dinh dang. Tuong tu Ctrl+; chen ngay (serial ngay + dinh dang ngay) - da dung. Lam: Ctrl+Shift+; luu serial + numFmt gio; cap nhat 2 bai kiem cu (mong doi chuoi HH:mm:ss) voi BANG CHUNG: (a) tai lieu hanh vi Excel 'Ctrl+Shift+; = nhap gio hien tai' dang gia tri thoi gian; (b) app ghi XLSX -> Excel 2016 doc lai o do: Value2 la so (0<x<1) va NumberFormat la dinh dang gio -> khop cach Excel luu o thoi gian; ghi vao docs/PLAN-EXCEL-2016-E3.md.
