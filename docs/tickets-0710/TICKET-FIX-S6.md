3. Dropdown "So": Excel Number = "0.00" (KHONG co dau phan cach nghin; hien "1234.50"); app dung "#,##0.00" ("1,234.50"). Doi "So" mac dinh thanh 0.00; cap "Dau phay" (Comma Style, nut Ctrl+Shift+1 = "#,##0.00") thanh nut/lenh rieng neu da co (kiem id lenh sheet.format.thousands/comma: hien NO_BUTTON trong test — tim lenh dung va nut tren ribbon).

Tu gian cot (khi ap dinh dang so/ngay/tien te khien chuoi hien thi dai hon cot, chi khi cot khong bi nguoi dung dat rong tuy y — Excel chi tu gian neu cot dang o rong mac dinh).
