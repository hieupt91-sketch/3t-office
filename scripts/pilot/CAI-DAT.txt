VN OFFICE - BAN DUNG THU NOI BO
================================

Day la ban dung thu. Muc dich: dung that hang ngay de tim loi truoc khi phat hanh chinh thuc.

YEU CAU MAY
- Windows 10 hoac 11, 64-bit, trong o dia con it nhat 4 GB.
- Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (thuong da co san tren Windows 10/11).
- Khong can Internet de su dung. Khong can quyen quan tri (Administrator).

CAI DAT
1. Giai nen toan bo thu muc VNOffice-... ra may (vi du ra Desktop).
2. Bam chuot phai vao tep install-pilot.ps1 > "Run with PowerShell".
   (Hoac mo PowerShell trong thu muc do va chay:
    powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File install-pilot.ps1 )
3. Doi chep xong (vai phut). Tren Desktop va Start Menu se co:
   VN Writer (soan van ban, thay Word), VN Sheet (bang tinh, thay Excel), VN Slides (trinh chieu).
4. Sau khi cai xong co the xoa thu muc vua giai nen.

Ban dung thu KHONG thay doi chuong trinh mo tep mac dinh: nhap dup tep .docx/.xlsx van mo bang Microsoft Office.
Muon mo bang VN Office: mo VN Writer / VN Sheet roi dung Ctrl+O.

LUU Y KHI DUNG THU
- LUON GIU BAN GOC tai lieu quan trong. Nen dung "Luu thanh" (Ctrl+Shift+S) ra tep moi khi thu.
- Tep Excel rat lon (hang chuc nghin dong, nhieu cong thuc) co the mo cham vai phut - day la loi da biet, dang toi uu.
- Lan dau mo, ung dung se hoi co dong y gui BAO CAO LOI tu dong hay khong.
  Bao cao chi gom thong tin ky thuat (loai loi, thao tac, phien ban). KHONG BAO GIO gui noi dung tai lieu hay ten tep.
  Co the bat/tat lai trong tab Tro giup.
- Gap loi hoac thay gi chua giong Word/Excel: vao tab Tro giup > "Bao loi / Gop y", mo ta ngan gon.

GO CAI DAT
Start Menu > VN Office > "Go cai dat VN Office".
